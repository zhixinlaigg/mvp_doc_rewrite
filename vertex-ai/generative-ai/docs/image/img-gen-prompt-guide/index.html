Prompt and image attribute guide¶

To use Imagen on Vertex AI, you provide a text description, or prompt, of the image you want to generate or edit. Prompts are the primary way you communicate your intent to the generative model.

This guide explains how to structure your prompts to achieve different results, with examples of images you can create. It also provides guidance on how to refine your prompts through iteration to achieve your desired outcome.

Before you begin¶

Initial Setup and Guidelines

Product usage

To view usage standards and content restrictions associated with Imagen on Vertex AI, see the usage guidelines.

Content filtering

Generated images are filtered for undesirable or harmful content. Similarly, any input Imagen on Vertex AI receives is checked for offensive content, including text prompts and uploaded photos for image editing. For more information, see Responsible AI and usage guidelines for Imagen.

You can also report suspected abuse of Imagen on Vertex AI or any generated output that contains inappropriate material or inaccurate information using the Report suspected abuse on Google Cloud form.

Concepts
  • Prompt: A natural language text description of the image you want to generate or edit.
  • Negative Prompt: A text description of what you want to exclude from the generated image.
  • Aspect Ratio: The proportional relationship between the width and height of the image.
  • Parameterization: A technique to create prompt templates with variables that can be dynamically populated, useful for building applications on top of Imagen.

Troubleshooting

If you encounter issues with code snippets or setup, refer to the Troubleshooting Guide.

Prompting fundamentals¶

While there's no single way to write a perfect prompt, most effective prompts are descriptive, clear, and structured around a few key elements. A good starting point is to think in terms of subject, context, and style.

  • Subject: The main object, person, animal, or scenery you want an image of.
  • Context and background: The environment or setting where the subject is placed, such as a studio with a white background, outdoors, or an indoor scene.
  • Style: The artistic look of the image. Styles can be general (e.g., painting, photograph, sketch) or very specific (e.g., pastel painting, charcoal drawing, isometric 3D).

After writing an initial prompt, refine it by adding more details. Iteration is key. Start with a core idea, and then expand upon it until the generated image matches your vision.

Prompt: A park in the spring next to a lake Prompt: A park in the spring next to a lake, the sun sets across the lake, golden hour Prompt: A park in the spring next to a lake, the sun sets across the lake, golden hour, red wildflowers

Core prompting techniques¶

Beyond the fundamentals, you can use several techniques to gain more control over the generated images.

Write prompts for Imagen 3¶

View Imagen for Generation model card

Imagen 3 can transform your ideas into detailed images from both short and long prompts. You can refine your vision through iterative prompting, adding details until you achieve the perfect result.

Short prompts let you generate an image quickly. Prompt: close-up photo of a woman in her 20s, street photography, movie still, muted orange warm tones Longer prompts let you add specific details and build your image. Prompt: captivating photo of a woman in her 20s utilizing a street photography style. The image should look like a movie still with muted orange warm tones.

Additional advice for Imagen 3 prompt writing:

  • Use descriptive language: Employ detailed adjectives and adverbs to paint a clear picture for Imagen 3.
  • Provide context: If necessary, include background information to aid the AI's understanding.
  • Reference specific artists or styles: If you have a particular aesthetic in mind, referencing specific artists or art movements can be helpful.
  • Enhance facial details: To improve facial details in personal or group images, specify it as a focus (e.g., use the word "portrait") and consider using a larger model like Imagen 3 over Imagen 3 Fast.

Generate text in images¶

Imagen 3 can render text within your images. Use the following guidance to get the most out of this feature:

  • Iterate with confidence: You might need to regenerate images a few times to achieve the look you want.
  • Keep it short: Limit text to 25 characters or less for optimal results.
  • Use multiple phrases: Experiment with two or three distinct phrases. Avoid exceeding three phrases for cleaner compositions.
  • Guide placement: While you can direct text placement, expect some variations as this feature improves.
  • Inspire font style: Specify a general font style (e.g., "bold font") to influence the output.
  • Suggest font size: Indicate a general size (e.g., small, medium, large) to influence the generated font size.

Use negative prompts¶

You can provide a negative prompt along with your main prompt to specify what elements to omit from the image. This is a powerful tool for refining your output.

Recommended: Plainly describe what you don't want. For example, "wall, frame".

Not recommended: Avoid instructive language like "no" or "don't". For example, avoid phrases like "no walls" or "don't show walls".

Prompt (no negative prompt): 4K video game concept art, urban jungle, cyberpunk city, detailed rendering Prompt: 4K video game concept art, urban jungle, cyberpunk city, detailed rendering
Negative prompt: greenery, plants, forest, trees
Prompt (no negative prompt): Illustration of a mythical wyvern flying over mountains Prompt: Illustration of a mythical wyvern flying over mountains
Negative prompt: snow, frost

Control the aspect ratio¶

Imagen 3 lets you set five distinct image aspect ratios.

  • Square (1:1, default): A standard square photo, common for social media posts.
  • Fullscreen (4:3): Commonly used in media, film, and older TVs. It captures more of the scene horizontally compared to 1:1.
  • Portrait full screen (3:4): The fullscreen ratio rotated 90 degrees, capturing more of the scene vertically.
  • Widescreen (16:9): The standard for modern TVs, monitors, and landscape mobile phone screens. Ideal for scenic landscapes.
  • Portrait (9:16): Widescreen rotated 90 degrees, popularized by short-form video. Use for tall subjects like buildings or waterfalls.
Prompt: close up of a musician's fingers playing the piano, black and white film, vintage (4:3 aspect ratio) Prompt: a man wearing all white clothing sitting on the beach, close up, golden hour lighting (16:9 aspect ratio) Prompt: a digital render of a massive skyscraper, modern, grand, epic with a beautiful sunset in the background (9:16 aspect ratio)

Parameterize prompts¶

When building applications with the Imagen API or Vertex AI SDK for Python, you can parameterize prompts to control user inputs. For example, to let customers generate a logo, you can create a template like this:

A {logo_style} logo for a {company_area} company on a solid color background. Include the text {company_name}.

Your application's UI can then populate the {variables} based on user selections.

Example 1: A minimalist logo for a health care company on a solid color background. Include the text Journey.

Example 2: A modern logo for a software company on a solid color background. Include the text Silo.

Advanced prompting: Styles and modifiers¶

Use the following examples to create more specific prompts by adding modifiers for photography, art styles, materials, and image quality.

Photographic styles

To generate a photograph, start your prompt with keywords like "A photo of...".

Prompt: A photo of coffee beans in a kitchen on a wooden surface Prompt: A photo of a chocolate bar on a kitchen counter Prompt: A photo of a modern building with water in the background

Photography modifiers

You can add photography-specific modifiers to further refine your image.

  • Camera Proximity: Close up, taken from far away | Prompt: A close-up photo of coffee beans | Prompt: A zoomed out photo of a small bag of coffee beans in a messy kitchen | | :--- | :--- |
  • Camera Position: aerial, from below | Prompt: aerial photo of urban city with skyscrapers | Prompt: A photo of a forest canopy with blue skies from below | | :--- | :--- |
  • Lighting: natural, dramatic, warm, cold | Prompt: studio photo of a modern arm chair, natural lighting | Prompt: studio photo of a modern arm chair, dramatic lighting | | :--- | :--- |
  • Camera Settings: motion blur, soft focus, bokeh, portrait | Prompt: photo of a city with skyscrapers from the inside of a car with motion blur | Prompt: soft focus photograph of a bridge in an urban city at night | | :--- | :--- |
  • Lens types: 35mm, 50mm, fisheye, wide angle, macro | Prompt: photo of a leaf, macro lens | Prompt: street photography, new york city, fisheye lens | | :--- | :--- |
  • Film types: black and white, polaroid | Prompt: a polaroid portrait of a dog wearing sunglasses | Prompt: black and white photo of a dog wearing sunglasses | | :--- | :--- |

Artistic and illustrative styles

Art styles can range from monochrome sketches to hyper-realistic digital art.

Example prompt: "An [art style or creation technique] of an angular sporty electric sedan with skyscrapers in the background"

Prompt: A technical pencil drawing of an angular... Prompt: A charcoal drawing of an angular... Prompt: A color pencil drawing of an angular...
Prompt: A pastel painting of an angular... Prompt: A digital art of an angular... Prompt: An art deco (poster) of an angular...

Historical art references

You can reference iconic art periods or movements in your prompt.

Example prompt: "generate an image in the style of [art period or movement]: a wind farm"

Prompt: generate an image in the style of an impressionist painting: a wind farm Prompt: generate an image in the style of a renaissance painting: a wind farm Prompt: generate an image in the style of pop art: a wind farm
Object-based styles

You can create imagery that is otherwise difficult or impossible by specifying shapes and materials.

Prompt: a duffle bag made of cheese Prompt: neon tubes in the shape of a bird Prompt: an armchair made of paper, studio photo, origami style
Improve image quality

Certain keywords can signal to the model that you're looking for a high-quality asset.

  • General Modifiers: high-quality, beautiful, stylized
  • Photos: 4K, HDR, Studio Photo
  • Art, Illustration: by a professional, detailed
Prompt (no quality modifiers): a photo of a corn stalk Prompt (with quality modifiers): 4k HDR beautiful photo of a corn stalk taken by a professional photographer
