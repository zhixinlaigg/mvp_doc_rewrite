Cost of building and deploying AI models in Vertex AI¶

This page covers pricing for Generative AI on Vertex AI. Prices are listed in US Dollars (USD). If you pay in a currency other than USD, the prices listed in your currency on Cloud Platform SKUs apply.

You're charged only for requests that return a 200 OK response code. Requests returning other response codes, such as 4xx and 5xx, are not charged.

For all other Vertex AI pricing, including ML Platform and MLOps services, see the Vertex AI pricing page.

Concepts
  • Token: The basic unit of data (text, image, etc.) processed by a model; for text, one token is approximately four characters.
  • Modality: The type of data input or output, such as text, image, audio, or video.
  • Grounding: The process of connecting a model's response to verifiable sources, like Google Search or your own data.
  • Context Caching: A feature that stores parts of an input to reduce cost and latency on subsequent, similar requests.
  • Provisioned Throughput: A service that guarantees a specific level of model processing capacity for a fixed price.

Gemini Models¶

The billing method for Gemini models depends on the model version. Newer models (Gemini 2.x) are billed per token, while legacy models (Gemini 1.x) are billed per character and modality (e.g., per image, per second of video).

Model Family Primary Billing Unit
Gemini 2.x Tokens: Billed per 1 million input and output tokens.
Gemini 1.x Characters & Modalities: Billed per 1,000 characters, per image, or per second of audio/video.

Provisioned Throughput¶

Provisioned throughput guarantees processing capacity for your models, billed via generative AI scale units (GSUs). Learn how much throughput each GSU provides here and use our online estimator.

Duration Price per GSU Per
1 week commit $1,200 Week
1 month commit $2,700 Month
3 month commit $2,400 Month
1 year commit $2,000 Month
Example cost calculation

A user needs to support 10 queries per second (QPS) on gemini-2.0-flash with an input of 1,000 text tokens and 500 audio tokens, and an output of 300 text tokens.

1. Calculate burndown-adjusted tokens per query: Using the burndown rate table: * Input: (1,000 text tokens * 1) + (500 audio tokens * 7) = 4,500 adjusted input tokens. * Output: (300 text tokens * 4) = 1,200 adjusted output tokens. * Total: 4,500 + 1,200 = 5,700 adjusted tokens per query.

2. Calculate total tokens per second: * 5,700 tokens/query * 10 QPS = 57,000 tokens per second.

3. Calculate required GSUs: * 57,000 tokens/sec ÷ 3,360 throughput/GSU = 16.96 GSUs. * The user must purchase 17 GSUs (rounded up).

4. Calculate total cost: * 1 week: 17 GSUs * $1,200/GSU = $20,400 * 1 month: 17 GSUs * $2,700/GSU = $45,900 * 1 year: 17 GSUs * $2,000/GSU/month = $34,000 per month
