Models supported by Model Garden¶

Model Garden provides access to a wide variety of foundation models from Google, open-source providers, and third-party partners. These models are discoverable in the Vertex AI Model Garden and can be used for various generative AI tasks.

To help you choose the right model, the following table summarizes the different types of models available.

Model Category Description Key Characteristics
Google's first-party models State-of-the-art, proprietary models developed by Google. - Fully managed and optimized for Google Cloud.
- Enterprise-grade support and reliability.
- Includes flagship models like Gemini and Imagen.
Open-source models Popular open-source models with provided notebooks for deployment, tuning, and serving on Vertex AI. - Offers flexibility and control over the model and infrastructure.
- Requires you to manage deployment and serving.
- Includes models like Llama, Gemma, and Stable Diffusion.
Partner models Models from leading AI companies, available as managed APIs on Vertex AI. - Billed through your Google Cloud account.
- No need to manage the underlying infrastructure.
- Provides access to specialized models from partners like Anthropic and Mistral AI.
